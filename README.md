# 🏨 Hotel Booking Cancellation Analysis  
*Data Analytics Case Study — Portugal Hotel*

---

## 📌 Project Overview  
Customer booking cancellations are one of the biggest challenges in the hotel industry directly impacting **revenue**, **room management**, and **operational efficiency**.  

This project aims to **analyze hotel booking data** from Portugal to identify the main factors influencing customer cancellations and provide **data-driven recommendations** to minimize them.

---

## 🎯 Objectives  
1. Analyze patterns and behaviors that lead to booking cancellations.  
2. Explore how **lead time**, **special requests**, **deposit types**, and **customer segments** affect cancellation rates.  
3. Deliver actionable insights and **strategic recommendations** to help hotel management reduce cancellations and improve customer retention.

---

## 📊 Key Insights  
- 🔹 **56%** of total bookings were canceled (≈19,000 cancellations in 3 years).  
- 💳 Bookings with **No Deposit** and **Non-Refund** policies showed the highest cancellation rates.  
- 🙅‍♂️ Customers with **no special requests** were more likely to cancel their bookings.  
- 👥 **Group bookings** had the highest cancellation percentage, followed by **Offline Travel Agents (TA/TO)**.  
- 🚗 Customers who did **not request parking** also showed higher cancellation tendencies.

---

## 💡 Recommendations  
- ✉️ **Send reminder emails** to customers who book far in advance (long lead time) to reduce uncertainty.  
- 🎁 Offer **special request bundles** at lower prices to increase booking commitment.  
- 💵 Apply a **refundable deposit system with a specific refund deadline** to maintain customer flexibility while encouraging stronger booking commitment.  
- 🤝 Strengthen coordination with **Travel Agents (TA/TO)** and provide incentives for confirmed bookings.  
- 📍 Track customers’ transportation preferences to improve parking-related booking management.
